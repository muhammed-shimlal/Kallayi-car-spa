# 🚗 Kallayi Car Spa - Features

## Overview

Kallayi Car Spa is a Vehicle Wash Management System designed to streamline the daily operations of a car wash business. It provides secure management of customers, vehicles, services, staff, bookings, and financial records through a modern Django REST API.
