# 🗺️ Kallayi Car Spa Development Roadmap

## Overview

This roadmap outlines the planned development of the **Kallayi Car Spa Vehicle Wash Management System**. It provides a high-level view of completed work, current priorities, and future enhancements.

> **Note:** Features marked as **Completed** should reflect the current implementation in the repository. Update their status as development progresses.
